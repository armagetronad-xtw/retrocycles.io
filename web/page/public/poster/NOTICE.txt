scene.avif, scene.webp: the play page's poster (index.html, App.tsx, page.css ".poster")

What it is: the attract scene's opening frame as the game draws it (this repository's wasm
client: the 3D stadium, depth of field, the graphics line; the default
replay, and textures/dir_wall.png at alpha ~236), captured at 1920x1080 from the dev page
with the page's own layers hidden (#root display:none) 250 ms after the first `scene`
event. The replay opens on the same clip and camera on every load, so the live scene takes
over from the poster on the same picture. The models and textures are the game's (GPL, see
/licenses/).

Encoded: scene.avif (ImageMagick, -quality 66, 1920x1080, ~27 KB), scene.webp (-resize
1600x900 -quality 70, ~31 KB) for browsers without AVIF.

Re-capture it when the replay's first clip, its camera or the game's look change
(src/tron/gSceneReplayData.h, the SCENE_REPLAY_CAMERA_* and SCENE_REPLAY_CINEMA_* camera, the
stadium, the wall and floor look).
